# Project Background
The company is a renowned Japanese multinational corporation specializing in seasoning products. As part of its strategic expansion, the company initiated a project to develop its market presence on E-commerce platforms in Vietnam which included **Shopee, Lazada, Tiktok and Tiki**, aiming to better reach and serve local consumers.

Although the company has significant amount of data across its sales, marketing campaigns, product offerings, and loyalty programs, these have been not optimally utilized for strategic decision-making. To address this gap, this project was established to synthesis data from multiple E-commerce marketing sources (all above e-commerce platforms), and to uncover actionable business insights that drive cost optimization. 

Some key insights and recommendations are provided in these points:
**- GMV (Gross Merchandise Value) and Expense (Voucher fees) Trends Analysis**: Evaluation of historical patterns of 3 types of vouchers: Vouchers for Order (Order voucher), Vouchers for each SKU products (SKU voucher), and Subsidized platform voucher.


By establishing a framework to accurately measure campaign effectiveness across various platforms and evaluating actual performance against initial marketing plans, the project empowers the business to maximize its marketing ROI and streamline its E-commerce operations.
